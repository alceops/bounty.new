# OSS Bounty Radar — buyer decision page (2026-04-30)

A public-data-only queue cleanup report for maintainers, OSS sponsors, and engineering leads who want a short, source-linked view of where paid help is most likely to unblock work.

## What you get

| Package | Price hypothesis | Output | Best fit |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Lightweight shortlist | `$49` one-off | 5-10 public issues/opportunities with freshness, owner activity, duplicate/crowding risk, and source URLs | A sponsor or maintainer wants a small bounty shortlist before committing budget |
| Repo cleanup readout | `$100-$150` one-off | Queue map for one public repo: stale/crowded/unassigned/high-signal issues, unsafe exclusions, and recommended cleanup/bounty actions | A repo owner wants to turn backlog noise into paid or contributor-ready work |
| Monthly refresh | `$149/mo` hypothesis | Repeatable refresh for one repo/niche with change notes and newly qualified items | A team wants ongoing public backlog monitoring |

No private data, no credentials, no scraping around access controls, no security/vulnerability issue amplification by default, and no payment collection inside GitHub issues. A request is only a fit check until both sides agree scope and payment route.

## Buy/fit-check route

If this would save you time, use one of these low-friction routes:

1. Open a [custom data request](https://github.com/alceops/bounty.new/issues/new?template=oss-bounty-radar-custom-request.yml) with the repo/niche and the output you might pay for.
2. Open a quick [BUYER / BUILDER / NO signal](https://github.com/alceops/bounty.new/issues/new?template=oss-bounty-radar-signal.yml) if you only want to validate or reject the idea.
3. Email [alce.ops@gmail.com](mailto:alce.ops@gmail.com?subject=OSS%20Bounty%20Radar%20buyer%20fit%20check) with a public repo URL and the price tier that would be acceptable.

## Kill conditions

This route should be replaced, not passively watched, if there are still zero buyer/custom-request issues and no email reply after the next distribution check. The next replacement should either target a named buyer segment with a narrower sample or attach a paid-scope deliverable to an already-active direct-service conversation.
